1. BUSINESS & BRAND
   What does your business do, and who is your primary customer?
   Naturesip (Pty) Ltd is a premium eco-friendly retail kiosk and shop footprint specializing in a multi-stage
   purified water refilling station, seamlessly integrated with a high-margin fresh fruit slicing bar and a
   premium fruit slushy station. Our primary customers are urban and suburban South African shoppers,
   mall visitors, families, and health-conscious youth looking for affordable, high-quality, and refreshing
   visual and nutritional treats.
   What problem does your website need to solve for the business?
   The website must solve three critical problems: build local and regional brand awareness surrounding our
   flagship store launch in Soweto, educate customers on our rigorous multi-stage filtration transparency to
   build trust, and anchor our "Phygital" Loyalty Funnel by driving web traffic directly into our automated
   WhatsApp CRM framework via digital scanning structures and localized promotions.
   Do you have an existing brand (logo, colours, fonts)? Can I see it?
   Yes, our baseline brand concept is established as outlined in the Naturesip Business Plan. The visual
   identity incorporates premium-tier clean water imagery combined with vibrant fruit-centric aesthetics
   (countertop displays, clear packaging, and LED front lightboxes). Full brand assets, window vinyl
   specifications, and menu boards are ready to be integrated into the web interface.
   How would you describe your brand personality in 3 words?
   Transparent, Refreshing, Innovative.
   Naturesip (Pty) Ltd — Digital Strategy & Web Discovery 1
   Who are your main competitors, and which of their sites do you admire or dislike?
   Our main indirect competitors are traditional supermarket chains selling expensive bottled water in singleuse
   plastics, as well as localized, low-experience water refill setups. We dislike the cold, unengaging, and
   transactional nature of traditional water depot websites. We admire modern, high-vibrancy consumer
   lifestyle brand sites that emphasize experiential health, visual flavor customization, and instant
   community rewards.
   Is there a site outside your industry that captures the look and feel you want?
   Premium, modern quick-service food chains or cold-pressed juice bars that combine ultra-clean minimal
   backdrops with highly colorful, high-definition product imagery, clear transparent messaging, and instant
   digital loyalty loops.
2. BUSINESS GOALS
   What is the primary business goal for this site — leads, sales, bookings, awareness?
   The primary goal is Awareness and Digital Loyalty Lead Generation. The site must serve as the digital
   storefront that channels local mobile web traffic into our automated WhatsApp Business pipeline, driving
   physical footfall to the Soweto retail kiosk and securing immediate recurring customer habits.
   What does success look like 6 months after launch? How would you measure it?
   Success is a highly engaged local digital community. This will be measured by tracking localized geofenced
   traffic, the volume of digital loyalty stamp card signups via the web-to-WhatsApp pipeline, and
   customer acquisition metrics during promotional launch weeks.
   Are there any secondary goals the site should support? e.g. recruitment, press, partner
   acquisition
   Yes, a critical secondary goal is Partner and Expansion Acquisition. Since Naturesip is designed as an
   optimized turnkey model with an immediate expansion strategy targeting major urban/suburban retail
   giants across Johannesburg, the site must feature a professional investor/franchise portal to support
   multi-site rollout options.
   Is there a specific launch date or deadline driving this project?
   The digital launch is tightly coordinated with the physical deployment and fit-out of our anchor flagship
   kiosk in the Soweto mall plaza.
   Naturesip (Pty) Ltd — Digital Strategy & Web Discovery 2
   What is your budget range for this project?
   The initial capital expenditure for the operational turnkey setup is structured at R107,100 per site, with
   marketing, branding, and digital funneling integrated systematically into this rollout model.
3. TARGET AUDIENCE & USERS
   Who is the primary user of the site? Describe them as specifically as you can.
   Our primary users are busy urban and suburban consumers, families seeking relief from erratic municipal
   setups and expensive bottled water, and the vibrant youth market attracted to healthy, visual treats like
   our custom fruit slushies and fresh slicing bar. They are digitally native and highly active on social
   platforms like Meta and TikTok.
   What devices will most of your users visit from — mobile, desktop, or both?
   Over 90% of visits will come from Mobile Devices, specifically users clicking through hyper-local geofenced
   ads on TikTok/Instagram, or scanning physical QR codes embedded on our clear cups,
   packaging, menus, and flyers in and around the mall plaza.
   What does a user need to accomplish when they land on the site? Map the critical path before
   designing the nav.
   The path is short and high-converting: 1) Verify the proximity of the kiosk and see operating hours, 2)
   View our highly transparent water purity/TDS metrics and vibrant fruit menu, and 3) Click the main call-toaction
   button to enter our automated WhatsApp pipeline to claim an instant launch promotion or digital
   stamp card.
   What do first-time visitors need to understand within the first 5 seconds?
   They must instantly understand that Naturesip delivers premium-tier, multi-stage purified water at a
   fraction of supermarket costs, alongside healthy, delicious fresh fruit slices and slushies at a beautifully
   clean, eco-friendly kiosk right in their community.
   Are there secondary audiences (e.g. investors, press) the site should serve?
   Yes, property managers from commercial retail centers and prospective expansion investors looking to
   license or fund the next turnkey turnkey location across Gauteng.
   Naturesip (Pty) Ltd — Digital Strategy & Web Discovery 3
4. DESIGN & AESTHETIC
   What adjectives should the design feel — minimal, bold, playful, luxe, editorial?
   The design should feel Bold, Fresh, Pure, and Vibrant. It must balance pristine clinical cleanliness
   (reflecting our sediment, carbon, reverse osmosis, and UV sterilization standards) with energetic, highmargin
   visual elements that appeal to a lifestyle and youth market.
   Are there any colours, styles, or design trends you specifically want to avoid?
   We strictly avoid muted, dull industrial greys or dark tones that lack vitality. We also avoid overly cluttered,
   text-heavy corporate websites that look outdated and unapproachable.
   Should the design feel consistent with your current brand, or is this a rebrand moment?
   It must be completely consistent with our physical kiosk profile. The website should mirror the physical
   store layout, featuring our LED lightboxes, clear transparent aesthetics, and high-quality fruit visuals to
   provide a smooth omni-channel experience.
   Do you need photography or video, or do you have existing assets?
   We will leverage video assets and storefront imagery designed for our hyper-local Meta and TikTok paid
   social campaigns, illustrating the purification process and the live creation of fresh fruit salads.
5. FEATURES & FUNCTIONALITY
   What pages does the site need? Do you have a sitemap or page list in mind?
   A high-efficiency, multi-section single-page architecture is preferred for mobile speed, consisting of: 1)
   Hero Hub (Vibrant concept introduction), 2) Purity Lab (Visual breakdown of the filtration system and daily
   live TDS metrics), 3) The Menu (Pure Refills, Slushies, Slicing Bar), 4) Location & Hours, and 5) B2B
   Expansion Portal (Secure investor inquiry form).
   Does the site need a content management system so you can update it yourself?
   Yes, a lightweight, user-friendly CMS is required so that staff or management can instantly update daily
   water purity TDS metrics from a smartphone, ensuring real-time customer transparency.
   Naturesip (Pty) Ltd — Digital Strategy & Web Discovery 4
   Do you need e-commerce, payment processing, or product listings?
   We need clear visual product listings for our core pillars. Physical retail processing is fully covered instore
   via our tap-and-go POS systems, but the website requires digital loyalty integration that syncs
   directly with the WhatsApp CRM pipeline.
   Do you need SEO, analytics dashboards, or tracking from day one?
   Yes. Hyper-local local SEO is critical so that anyone searching for water refills, healthy snacks, or pure
   water in Soweto/Johannesburg finds Naturesip instantly. We also require robust tracking pixels (Meta &
   TikTok) to measure conversion rates from our paid social media campaigns.
6. CONTENT & COPY
   Who is writing the copy — you, me, or a third party?
   The copy will be collaborative, extracting core values, equipment parameters, and strategic frameworks
   directly from our approved Naturesip Business Plan to ensure 100% brand consistency.
   Will the site need to be updated regularly? Who will maintain it post-launch?
   The core layout will remain stable, while operational metrics like live TDS values and seasonal fruit
   specials will be updated dynamically via management's smartphone interface.
7. TECHNICAL & DELIVERY
   Are there any tech stack preferences or constraints I should know about?
   The platform must be hyper-optimized for mobile loading speed and light data consumption, ensuring
   smooth access for South African consumers browsing via mobile networks. It must support reliable
   external API routing to instantly trigger our automated WhatsApp Business chat flows upon user scanning
   or button selection.
